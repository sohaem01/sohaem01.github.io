Assembly Making

From using the materials given in the beginning of this project, we have build a prototype of our robot. Although we haven't been able to completely attach all the components properly (soldering, casings), we used tape as a way to make sure all of the necessary components were attached to the bed of the robot.

Initially, we were unsure exactly how we should place all of the batteries, breadboards, esp32, and the wiring was a mess as well. However, taking into consideration of exactly what we want to do with our robot helped a lot. We wanted to make sure that our photoresistors were able to get a clear vision of the path below it, as this is what really determines how well our robot is able to follow a line. We also didn't want there to be anything that is a possible obstruction to the wheel screwed to the bottom of the chassis as well as the gear motors to the side.

The pictures below showcase the finalized work that we have done to create our prototype.

As you can see, the main body of our robot consists of the components given to us and the use of tape to make sure that everything is able to fit all together. In the future, you'll see our progression from this to a better and finalize version.
